# Security

Please report security problems privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Do not open a public issue for them.

Useful details: what an attacker can do, the steps to reproduce it, and the Sotto version or commit you tested.

Sotto runs without a server, so the interesting parts are local: the Accessibility and microphone permissions, text pasting into other apps, the custom shell commands in Modes, the optional Local CLI enhancement, model downloads, and the opt-in Unix socket to Eclipse.

I maintain Sotto alone in my free time. I will answer as soon as I can and credit you in the fix unless you prefer not to be named.
